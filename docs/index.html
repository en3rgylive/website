The star

It begins 150 million kilometres away.

Every second, the sun turns four million tonnes of itself into light. One ray sets out for Earth.

The journey

Eight minutes later, it meets the atmosphere.

It breaks through the cloud. Even a heavy grey sky lets some of the light through.

The roof

And it lands on your roof.

Most roofs simply let it pass.

The module

Here, light becomes power.

Silicon cells turn each photon into current. Nothing moves, so there is little to wear out.

The inverter

The inverter makes it usable.

Direct current in. The 230 volt alternating current your home runs on, out.

The whole house

And the whole house wakes up.

Lights, fans, AC and the EV charger run on your own sun.
